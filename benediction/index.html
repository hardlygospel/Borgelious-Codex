    🕯 Benediction

    May your glass be full, and your reasons few. May the round land on someone else. May the kebab find you before the phone does. And may you wake remembering only the good parts — which the Codex assures you were many, and declines, firmly, to specify.

    Go now. Pour wisely. The Codex will not be repeating itself; it is fairly sure it already said all this once tonight.
