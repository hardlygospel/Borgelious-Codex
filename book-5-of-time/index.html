    ⧖ Book the Fifth — Of Time, and the Falsehood Thereof

    The Prophet declared this doctrine at an hour he could not name, to a room that could not contradict him.

    V:i — Time is a construct. The Prophet proved this at what was probably 3am, to a man who had not asked. And the man — to his eternal credit — did not disagree, for the man could not have told you the hour either.

    V:iv — Behold the clock upon the wall. It is a false idol.

    It shows a number. The number means nothing. Thou hast looked upon it four times this night and retained nothing. This is correct. The clock has no power in this place.

    V:ix — They will cry "Last Call" as though Time itself commands it. It does not. Last Call is a thing that men invented — and what men have invented, men may un-invent. The Prophet attempted this. The Prophet was asked to leave. This does not disprove the doctrine; it merely concludes the lesson.

    V:xii — One hour of the Night and one hour of the Morning are not the same hour. The Night hour is vast, and golden, and gone in a single breath. The Morning hour is small, and grey, and will not end. Any physicist who tells thee these are equal has simply not been to the places thou hast been.

    V:xv — Know the Great Permission: it is always, somewhere upon the turning of the world, five of the clock. This is not an excuse. This is geography. The Prophet was, in his fashion, a man of science.

    V:xxi — "I shall deal with it tomorrow," saith the disciple. Yet Tomorrow, when at last it arrives, is only Today wearing a hat — and Today has never dealt with anything in its life. Thus the matter is passed gently down the line, forever, and this passing-down is what the Codex calls Peace.

    V:∞ — "Five more minutes" is not a measure of Time. It is a measure of Hope. The two have never once agreed, and the Codex has stopped trying to introduce them.1

    1. The Codex was, at the time of writing, four minutes into a "five more minutes." It remains there. It is, in a sense, still being written. ↩
